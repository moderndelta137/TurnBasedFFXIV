### Style Reference

---



---

The game should use a **FF Record Keeper-style mobile pixel-art battle presentation**.

---



---

Visual characteristics:

---



---

- 2D pixel-art characters

---

- Enemy on the left

---

- Party on the right

---

- Vertical mobile RPG battle layout

---

- Fantasy command-window UI

---

- Readable HP / MP / TP bars

---

- Clear combat icons

---

- Simple but satisfying VFX

---

- Blue / crystal / fantasy-style command panels

---

- Gold or bright borders for important UI emphasis
